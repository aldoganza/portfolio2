# 📖 AMOURA - Romance Stories Platform

## What is AMOURA?

**AMOURA** is a modern web platform designed specifically for African romance writers and readers. It provides a space where writers can share their love stories in both **English** and **Kinyarwanda**, while readers can discover and enjoy captivating romance content.

---

## 🎯 Mission

To empower African storytellers by providing them with a free, accessible platform to write, publish, and share romance stories in their native languages.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### For Writers 📝
- **Episode Publishing** - Release stories in serialized episodes
- **Schedule Episodes** - Build anticipation with timed releases
- **Auto-Save** - Never lose your work
- **Writing Goals** - Track daily word count and progress
- **Writer Levels** - Grow from Beginner to Featured Writer
- **Real-Time Stats** - Monitor likes, views, and engagement

### For Readers 💕
- **Bilingual Content** - Stories in English and Kinyarwanda
- **Continue Reading** - Pick up where you left off
- **Follow Writers** - Get updates from favorite authors
- **Emotional Reactions** - Express feelings with emojis
- **Offline Reading** - Download stories for offline access
- **Episode Countdown** - See when next episodes drop

### Platform Features 🌟
- **Language Switcher** - Toggle between English (🇺🇸) and Kinyarwanda (🇷🇼)
- **Responsive Design** - Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **Apple-Inspired UI** - Beautiful, modern interface
- **PWA Support** - Install as mobile app
- **Free to Use** - No subscription fees

---

## 🌍 Languages Supported

1. **English** - Global language for wider reach
2. **Kinyarwanda** - Native language of Rwanda (using proper translations verified by native speakers)

## 💻 Technology
